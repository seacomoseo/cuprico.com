José María Tornero Puga is a qualified electrical and solar installer and co-founder of Cúprico. Based in San Pedro del Pinatar, he works across the Murcia region and the Vega Baja (Alicante), specialising in solar PV systems, battery storage and EV chargers. He works with both Spanish and international clients, speaks English, and handles the legalisation and paperwork so owners don't have to.
