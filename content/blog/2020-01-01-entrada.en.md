### Buttons

By wrapping the text that is **inside** a link in brackets (`[[example]](https://example.com/)`).

If there is a button at the beginning of the line, it and all those that exist will adjust in widths and margins (**recommended**):

[[Button 1]](#buttons)
[[Button 2]](#buttons)

If there is no button at the beginning of the line, it and the following ones will break to a new line, take up the full width and will have no margins (**not recommended**):

This is a button: [[Button 1]](#buttons) [[Button 2]](#buttons)

## Icons

Anywhere by adding as text the ID of the desired icon (which can be checked on the following button) wrapped between the `|` character:

[[Font Awesome Icons]](https://fontawesome.com/search?ic=brands).

Examples:

- `star`: ::star::
- `tag`: ::tag::
- `home`: ::house::
- Here is an icon ::brand:apple:: of the famous apple
- Below is a button with an icon:

[[::star:: Button]](#icons)
