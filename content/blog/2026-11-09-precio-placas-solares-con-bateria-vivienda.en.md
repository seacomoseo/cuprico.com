If you're asking installers what solar panels with a battery would cost on your villa in Spain and getting wildly different answers (or none at all), this guide is for you. It's a common frustration among international owners: UK-style "from £X" pricing doesn't translate well here, and Spanish quotes can be hard to compare when you're reading them from another country. Below we explain what really drives the price, and how to check a quote line by line before you sign.

## Why there's no single price (and no price per square metre)

Many owners start by searching for a price based on the size of the house. It makes sense, but the floor area tells us very little about the system a villa needs. Two identical villas on the same street can need very different installations:

- One is a holiday home used for six weeks in summer, with the pool pump running while it's empty.
- The other is lived in all year, with air conditioning in every room and an electric car charging every night.

The first may need a modest system and possibly no battery at all. The second needs more panels and a properly sized battery. **What matters is how much electricity you use, and when, not how big the house is.**

## What actually drives the price

### System size (kWp)

How many panels you need to cover the part of your consumption that's worth covering. It's worked out from your bills, not guessed. Oversizing raises the cost without raising your savings, because surplus exported to the grid is credited at a lower value than the electricity you buy.

### Battery capacity (kWh)

This is where off-the-shelf quotes most often get it wrong. Battery size should be based on **what the house uses in the evening and at night**, not on the number of panels. Too big and it never fills; too small and it runs out by nine o'clock. Many modern batteries are modular, so you can start smaller and add capacity later, which suits owners who might move here for longer in future.

### The inverter

A battery needs a hybrid inverter (or an extra unit to manage the battery). If you already have panels, the price changes a lot depending on whether your current inverter can be used. That's why an upgrade can't be quoted properly without seeing it.

![](/u/fotovoltaica-blog/inversor-deye-bateria-upower-pylontech-01.webp "Hybrid inverter and battery in a home solar system in Spain")

### Your roof

Mounting on a tiled roof, a flat gravel roof, a metal roof or a pergola all cost differently. Structure type, height, access, scaffolding or a crane, and the shade-free area all count.

### Single-phase or three-phase supply

Your electricity supply type determines which inverter and battery can be fitted. Larger villas with high demand are often three-phase, and the equipment is different.

### Backup during power cuts

If you want essentials (fridge, router, alarm, cameras) to keep running when the grid goes down, the system must be designed for it from the start, with backup-capable equipment and a separate circuit for those loads. For an owner watching the house from abroad, it's often a priority.

### Spanish legalisation and paperwork

Every system must be legalised: the installation certificate, notifications to the town hall and the distribution company, and registration of the self-consumption system. Requirements vary by municipality. A good quote includes all of it and says so clearly.

## How a holiday-home calendar changes the design

If you only spend part of the year here, the system shouldn't be designed like one for a full-time home. We look at what keeps running while the house is empty (pool pump, fridge, alarm, dehumidifier) and what changes when you arrive. That can mean fewer panels than you'd expect, and a battery that is either essential (long winter stays, backup for an empty house) or not worth it yet (a few weeks a year). We go into this in [solar panels on a holiday home in Spain](https://cuprico.com/en/blog/placas-solares-segunda-residencia/).

## How to compare quotes, even from abroad

A Spanish quote is called a *presupuesto*. Comparing two of them on the total alone is like comparing two cars by price without knowing the model. Check that each one states:

::check:: **Make and model** of the panels, inverter and battery.
::check:: **Total system size** in kWp and **battery capacity** in kWh.
::check:: **What's included**: mounting structure, cabling, protection, legalisation and commissioning.
::check:: **Labour warranty** and each manufacturer's equipment warranty, in writing.
::check:: **Installation time** and who handles the Spanish paperwork.
::check:: Whether **backup during power cuts** is included.
::check:: Whether **VAT (IVA)** is included in the total.

| In the quote | Clear | Vague |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Equipment | ::check:: Make and model of each item | ::close:: "Premium panels" |

## And how much will I save?

That depends on your consumption, your tariff and how much of your own production you use, so be wary of anyone quoting savings without seeing your bills. Tax deductions and local council discounts can reduce what you end up paying, but most depend on being a Spanish taxpayer and on your municipality. We cover them in our [guide to solar grants and deductions](https://cuprico.com/en/blog/ayudas-deducciones-placas-solares-2026/); always confirm with official sources or your tax adviser.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much do solar panels with a battery cost in Spain?

It depends on system size, battery capacity, the inverter, your roof and the paperwork. We don't give fixed prices without seeing the property; you get an itemised quote within 24 hours of the visit.

### Can the price be worked out from the size of the house?

No. Your consumption and when it happens determine the system, not the floor area.

### Is it cheaper to fit the battery now or later?

If a hybrid inverter goes in from the start, adding a battery later is straightforward. If not, the inverter may need replacing. We'll plan it around how you use the house.

### Do I need to be in Spain for the visit and the installation?

Not necessarily. We can coordinate access with a neighbour or key holder and handle quotes and paperwork remotely. You'll only need to sign the documents that require it.

### Does your quote include legalisation?

Yes, itemised. Make sure any quote you compare includes it too.
