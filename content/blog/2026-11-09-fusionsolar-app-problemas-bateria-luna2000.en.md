If you have solar panels with a Huawei inverter on your villa in Spain and the FusionSolar app shows "offline", a blank screen or nothing at all, this guide is for you. It is one of the most common messages we get from international owners: the system on the roof is working perfectly, but they have been watching it blind from the UK, Belgium or Scandinavia for months.

Below we explain how to install the FusionSolar app from official sources, why it stops showing data (it is rarely the panels), what to do if your original installer can't be reached, and how to use the app's own figures to decide whether a Huawei LUNA2000 battery is worth adding.

## What the FusionSolar app does for you

FusionSolar is Huawei's app and web platform for monitoring your solar system. If your inverter is Huawei, it shows you:

::check:: How much your panels produce today, this month and this year.
::check:: How much the house uses, and when.
::check:: How much you export to the grid and how much you buy from it.
::check:: Battery charge and discharge, if you have one.
::check:: Alerts when the inverter reports a fault or loses its connection.

For an owner abroad, it's how you spot a pump that won't stop or a system that has quietly stopped producing, without flying over.

## How to install the FusionSolar app safely

According to [Huawei's official FusionSolar app guide](https://support.huawei.com/enterprise/en/doc/EDOC1100273864/5eeb2586/downloading-and-installing-the-app), the download channels are:

- **iPhone:** the App Store, searching for "FusionSolar". It is published by Huawei Digital Power Technologies and, according to its [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/fusionsolar/id1529080383), needs iOS 13 or later.
- **Huawei phones:** AppGallery, Huawei's own app store.
- **Other Android phones:** Huawei's official download (the QR code Huawei provides or the link on its FusionSolar portal). Huawei recommends Android 8.0 or later.

**Don't download it from third-party download sites.** This app connects to your home energy system, so it should only come from an official source. If you're not sure which link is the right one, ask your installer or ask us and we'll send it to you.

To log in, you use **the owner account your installer created for you**. The system is registered from the installer's FusionSolar account, and the installer creates your access. If you were never given a username, don't register a new account yourself: you'll get an empty account with no system attached. Ask whoever installed it to create your owner access.

## FusionSolar not working: the usual problems

### "The app says offline or shows no data"

In most cases we see, the panels are producing normally. What has failed is **the inverter's internet connection**. The inverter sends its data to Huawei's platform over your home Wi-Fi (or a communication dongle). When that link drops, the app freezes on the last data it received.

The typical causes in holiday homes:

- The **router was replaced**, or you switched internet provider.
- The **Wi-Fi password was changed**, sometimes by a property manager or a holiday-let guest who reset the router.
- The inverter sits in a garage or plant room with a **weak Wi-Fi signal**.
- A **power cut** while the house was empty, after which the router or inverter didn't reconnect.

### "We changed the router"

This is the classic one. The inverter keeps looking for the old network name and password. It has to be set up again on the new Wi-Fi. It's done through the app with commissioning access, without touching any wiring, but it needs installer permissions. An installer can often sort it in a short visit, and we can coordinate access with your neighbour, key holder or property manager if you're not in Spain.

A tip for next time: when you change provider, if the new router can keep the same network name and password, the inverter will usually reconnect on its own.

### "I can't remember my password"

You can reset it from the app's login screen using the email linked to your account. The catch: if your installer registered the account with their own email (it happens more than you'd think), they will need to reset it or create a new owner account under your email.

### "My installer has closed down or doesn't answer"

This is the worry we hear most from expat owners, and it has a solution. We can check your Huawei system **even if we didn't install it**, confirm everything is working and leave the app running under your name and email, in English. We see this often on older systems in [La Zenia](https://cuprico.com/en/solar-panel-installers/la-zenia/) and around golf resorts such as [La Torre Golf](https://cuprico.com/en/solar-panel-installers/la-torre-golf/).

### "The app shows an inverter alarm"

If FusionSolar shows a fault alarm (not just a communication issue), take a screenshot of the message and send it to your installer. **Don't open the inverter or the consumer unit**, and don't ask a neighbour to "have a look". These systems carry high-voltage direct current, and this is work for a licensed installer only.

## Use FusionSolar to decide on a LUNA2000 battery

Here's the useful part. The app gives you the data you need to judge whether a battery makes sense for your house, instead of relying on a sales pitch.

Look at two figures over a few weeks, ideally in different seasons and both when you're in the house and when it's empty:

1. **How much you export to the grid during the day.** That's what your panels produce and the house doesn't use at that moment.
2. **How much you buy from the grid at night.** That's what you use after sunset.

| What FusionSolar shows | What it usually means |
| --- | --- |
| High daytime export and significant night-time imports | ::check:: A battery may make sense: it would store what you now export and use it in the evening |
| Little export, most solar used during the day | ::close:: You probably don't need one; more panels may come first |
| High export but very low night use (house often empty) | ::close:: A battery would sit idle; it may only make sense as backup during power cuts |

On iPhone, from the App Store. On Huawei phones, from AppGallery. On other Android phones, from Huawei's official download. Avoid third-party download sites.

Usually because the inverter has lost its internet connection: new router, changed Wi-Fi password or weak signal. The panels keep producing; the data just isn't reaching the app.
