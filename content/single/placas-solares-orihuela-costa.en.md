    title: Income tax deductions
    sub: If you pay for the installation in 2026
    md: |
      In 2026 there are two income tax (IRPF) deductions you can use if you put solar panels on your home:

      - **National: 10% of what you pay**, up to a base of €5,000 a year. Only payments made **before 31 December 2026** count. The battery counts too.
      - **Valencian Community: 40% if it's your main home and 20% if it's a second home**, up to a base of €8,800 per home per year. Whatever you can't use in one year carries over to the next four.

      For the Valencian one you need to be tax resident in the Valencian Community, the installation has to be in the self-consumption register, and you have to pay by bank transfer or card, never in cash. Your tax advisor will tell you how the two combine in your case.

      **Not tax resident in Spain?** Income tax deductions don't apply to you, but you can still ask for the IBI (council tax) discount if your town hall offers it. We explain it in [solar panels for non-residents](/en/blog/placas-solares-no-residente-espana/).

      We give you the invoice, the installation certificate and the paperwork your tax advisor needs to apply what you're entitled to.
